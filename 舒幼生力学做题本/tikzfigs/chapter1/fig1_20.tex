% 1-26 题 图1-20：直角三角形管道 + 阶梯形折线轨道
% 直角在 B（AB 竖直, BC 水平），AC 斜边
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.85, font=\small]
  \coordinate (A) at (0,3.5);
  \coordinate (B) at (0,0);
  \coordinate (C) at (4.5,0);
  % 直角三角形
  \draw[thick] (A) -- (B) -- (C) -- cycle;
  \node[above left] at (A) {$A$};
  \node[below left] at (B) {$B$};
  \node[below right] at (C) {$C$};
  % 直角标记
  \draw (0,0.35) -- (0.35,0.35) -- (0.35,0);
  % 阶梯形折线轨道
  \draw[thick, blue!60!black, dashed] (A) -- (1.5,3.5) -- (1.5,1.5) -- (3,1.5) -- (3,0) -- (C);
  % 边标注
  \node[left] at ($(A)!0.5!(B)$) {$L_1$};
  \node[below] at ($(B)!0.5!(C)$) {$L_2$};
  \node[above right] at ($(A)!0.5!(C)$) {$L_3$};
\end{tikzpicture}
